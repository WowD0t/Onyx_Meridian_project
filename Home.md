# Wiki home

The complete reference for **Gloamguise: False Facades** — a Minecraft Bedrock add-on about building secret bases that survive close inspection.

> **Current release** — v2.1.5 · Bedrock 1.26.40+ · no experimental toggles · one `.mcaddon` with both packs.

Gloamguise adds **10 items** that disguise themselves as the blocks around them: pass-through ghost walls, one-way spy glass, doors and trapdoors with independent halves, and torch-shaped levers that can open a vault from another room — without any redstone signal.
