Torch Levers

The switch that is not a switch: a normal lever wearing a torch costume.

gg:torch_lever / gg:*_remote2 min read7 sections

Wired

gg:torch_lever⧉ · gg:soul_torch_lever⧉

Outputs power 15 into dust, pistons, lamps and redstone doors. Behaves exactly like a vanilla lever — it just looks like a torch.

Wireless

gg:torch_lever_remote⧉ · gg:soul_torch_lever_remote⧉

Outputs nothing. Link it to one door and it opens that door through walls, from any distance, without a wire in sight.

The four gestures

GestureWhereResult
Shift + tapAny decoy door or trapdoorStores the link in the torch you are holding — place it anywhere
Shift + tapAn already placed wireless torch (holding a torch)Copies its link, so you can build a second remote for the same door
Shift + tap, empty handAn already placed wireless torchRemoves the link and frees the torch
TapA linked torchOpens or closes its door through walls — including a paired double door
Every action answers
A coloured message, a chat line and a short chime confirm whether the link took. A redstone-only door explains itself when you tap it empty-handed.

Sharing

Every player can link their own torch to the same door; all linked torches stay in sync. Links survive reloads and pack updates — they live in world dynamic properties, sharded.

Recipes: 07–10 · the four torch crafts. Power rules: Redstone & wireless.
