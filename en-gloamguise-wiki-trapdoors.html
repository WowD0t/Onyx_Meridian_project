Decoy Trapdoors

Hatches that vanish into the ground — a grass hatch is genuinely invisible until it opens.

gg:super_trapdoor / gg:super_trapdoor_rs1 min read4 sections

gg:super_trapdoor⧉ · gg:super_trapdoor_rs⧉

Mounts on the top or the bottom of the block space like a vanilla trapdoor, swings toward whoever opens it, and copies the frame around it — including grass.

Behaviour

  • Top or bottom mount, exactly like vanilla.
  • Swings toward the player who opens it.
  • Copies the surrounding frame, grass tint included.
  • Pairs with the same redstone / wireless rules as doors.
