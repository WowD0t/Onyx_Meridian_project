The studio

Systems, documentation and official channels.

Onyx Meridian Interactive Studio is an independent studio building gameplay systems for Minecraft Bedrock. The studio is the constant; each add-on is a separate project with its own page, wiki and repository.

Projects ship with official files, checksums and documentation. Usage terms are defined by OM Studio EULA v3.1.

Licence v3.1

One licence for every Studio Work.

OM Studio EULA v3.1 is general: it applies to add-ons, tools, art, documentation, website content and future projects. English is authoritative; Russian is an official translation.

✓ You may

  • Play in single-player, Realms and servers.
  • Record, stream and review, including monetised videos.
  • Link to official channels.
  • Make private edits and keep one backup.

✗ You may not

  • Re-upload, mirror, bundle or paywall files.
  • Port, fork, extract assets or publish derivatives.
  • Remove credits, markers or licence notices.
  • Use Works for AI training or datasets.

✉ You may request

  • Bundles, modpacks and server packs.
  • Group uploads, mirrors and release posts.
  • Translations, ports and commercial use.
  • Any other permission, in writing.

How permissions work

Default: prohibited. If you want to do something the licence does not expressly allow, send a clear written request. The rights holder replies with permission granted, permission granted with conditions, or denial. Silence is not approval.

  1. Describe who you are, the Work and version, and the exact requested action.
  2. State scope, platforms, duration, commercial terms and any file changes.
  3. Explain how authorship, licence and official channels will be credited.
  4. Wait for a written reply that names the Work, act, scope, conditions and duration.
