Decoy Block

The ghost wall you can walk through — the gateway item every other craft starts from.

gg:super_block2 min read6 sections

gg:super_block⧉

Tap any face of any block to place it. The new block takes the neighbour’s appearance and becomes a ghost: no collision box and light dampening 0, so nothing shows from outside and nothing blocks your movement.

Placement

  • Click any face — corners, ceilings and floors all work.
  • The decoy copies the block you tapped, not the space it occupies.
  • Placing a decoy onto another decoy inherits its resolved camouflage (the chain trick).

Placement, step by step

1
Choose a face
Tap any side of a wall, floor, or ceiling. Corners work too.
2
Copy happens immediately
The new block copies the tapped block, including log direction and grass tint.
3
Walk through
There is no collision box and light dampening is 0, so movement is free and no light leaks through the fake wall.

Good uses

  • A walk-through wall section beside real blocks.
  • A fake tunnel extended by the chain trick.
  • A hidden floor or ceiling entrance that matches grass tint.
  • A light-preserving disguise for a glowing block.
